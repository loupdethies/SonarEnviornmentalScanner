Sonar Environmental Scanner – Project Summary

**Project Description**
The “Sonar Environmental Scanner” (SES) is a USB-powered, modular, portable sonar-based environmental scanning system for mapping gardens and detecting wildlife. It is designed to connect to a computer for signal processing and advanced environmental analysis.

**Main Objectives**

* Environmental monitoring via sonar reflections
* Generating a map of trees, lawns and other objects
* Detecting animals (e.g. birds, bats) by their sounds

**Key Specifications**

* **Frequency ranges:**

  * Receiver: 40 kHz – 150 kHz
  * Transmitter: 40 kHz – 80 kHz
* **Sampling rate:** 1.5 Msamples/s
* **Connection:** USB-C (optional Bluetooth or Wi-Fi)
* **Power supply:** USB bus power or external power bank
* **Data processing:** Digitized data processed on PC/laptop in C or Python
* **Form factor:** Portable, no larger than DIN A5 – A4

**Important Hardware Components**

* **Microcontroller:** STM32F4 (STM32F411RET6)
* **USB interface:** FT601Q-B-T (USB 3.1 to FIFO)
* **Ultrasound transmitter:** MA40S4S (40 kHz)
* **Microphone:** SPU0410LR5H-QB (MEMS)
* **Power management:** STUSB4500QTR (USB power controller), LMR33630ADDAR (buck converter), LT1763CS8-2.5 (linear regulator)

**Open Questions & Next Steps**

* Final selection of frequency range and transducer design
* Design of modular interfaces for additional sensors (e.g. temperature, air pressure, GPS)
* Decision whether to perform signal processing on the microcontroller or on the PC
